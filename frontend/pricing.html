Frequently Asked Questions

How many properties can I add?

Free gives you 1 address lookup with no account. Starter supports up to 25 properties. Portfolio supports up to 250 properties with API access. Enterprise offers unlimited properties and custom integrations.

Where does the pickup schedule data come from?

We aggregate data from municipal open data portals, waste hauler APIs, and crowdsourced community reports. All data is verified through our consensus algorithm.

Can I embed the schedule in my tenant portal?

Yes. Portfolio and Enterprise plans include an embeddable widget and iframe that you can drop into any tenant portal, including AppFolio, Buildium, and custom portals.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. All plans include a free 14-day trial with full access. No credit card required to start.

Do you support bulk address lookups?

Portfolio and Enterprise plans include the bulk API endpoint. Upload a CSV or POST an array of addresses and get schedules for all of them in one call.
