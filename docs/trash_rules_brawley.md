# Brawley Trash Pickup Rules

## Service Provider
**Republic Services** (formerly Allied Waste Transportation Services)
- Customer Service: (877) 732-9253
- Exclusive franchise agreement with City of Brawley

## Official Sources
- City of Brawley Public Works: https://www.brawley-ca.gov/departments/public-works/
- City of Brawley Main Website: https://www.brawley-ca.gov/
- Public Works Department Phone: (760) 344-5800

## Pickup Zone System

### Zone Information Status
**Limited Public Documentation** - Zone-specific information not readily available online

### How to Find Your Pickup Day
1. Contact City of Brawley Public Works at (760) 344-5800
2. Contact Republic Services at (877) 732-9253
3. Check your waste service bill for schedule information
4. Visit city hall or public works office

## Collection Schedule

### Service Details
- **Provider**: Republic Services under exclusive franchise
- **Service Type**: Residential, commercial, and industrial solid waste and recyclables
- **Rate Information**: Rate increase effective January 1, 2025
- **Cart System**: Three-cart system (trash, recycling, green waste) - assumed based on regional standard
