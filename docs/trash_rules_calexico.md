# Calexico Trash Pickup Rules

## Service Provider
**Republic Services** (formerly Allied Waste Services)
- Contract with City of Calexico for garbage, curbside recycling, and yard waste collection

## Pickup Zone System

### Zone Information Status
**Zone-specific schedule not publicly documented** - Multi-day collection schedule exists

### Collection Window
- **Days**: Monday through Saturday
- **Hours**: 5:00 AM to 8:00 PM

### How to Find Your Pickup Day
Specific zone/day assignments not found in public documentation. Must:
1. Contact City of Calexico Public Works at (760) 768-2160
2. Contact Republic Services customer service
3. Check your service bill for schedule information

## Collection Schedule

### Service Details
- **Frequency**: Once per week (per residence)
- **System**: Three-can system
  - One cart for recyclable materials
  - One cart for green waste (yard waste)
  - One cart for refuse (trash)
- **Collection Window**: Monday-Saturday, 5:00 AM - 8:00 PM

### Bulky Item Pickup
- **Frequency**: Weekly free bulky item pickup
- **Scheduling**: Residents entitled to one free pickup per week

### Holiday Schedule
- Contact Republic Services for holiday schedule
- Likely follows standard Republic Services holiday calendar
