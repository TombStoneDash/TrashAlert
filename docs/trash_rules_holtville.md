# Holtville Trash Pickup Rules

## Service Provider
**CR&R Environmental Services**
- Customer Service: (877) 482-5656 (toll-free) / (800) 826-9677
- Contract billing through City of Holtville

## Official Sources
- City of Holtville Utilities: https://www.holtville.ca.gov/utilities/

- CR&R Holiday Schedule: https://crrwasteservices.com/holiday-schedule/

## Pickup Zone System

### Zone Information Status
**Not Publicly Documented** - Must contact CR&R for specific day assignment

### How to Find Your Pickup Day
1. Contact CR&R El Centro at (877) 482-5656
2. Contact general CR&R customer service at (800) 826-9677
3. Check your utility bill for schedule information

## Collection Schedule

### Service Details
- **System**: Three-cart automated system (96-gallon carts with lids and wheels)
  - **Black container**: Regular refuse
  - **Blue container**: Recyclables
  - **Green container**: Green waste (yard waste, organic materials)
- **Collection Frequency**: Weekly (specific day varies by location)

### Holiday Schedule (2025)
CR&R observes the following holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Day

**Holiday Delay Policy**:
- If holiday falls on **Saturday or Sunday**: Normal collection, no delays
- If holiday falls on **weekday**: Collection for remainder of week postponed by one day
  - Example: If Independence Day is Monday and your normal day is Thursday, collection moves to Friday

### Free Dump Access
Holtville residents may dump for free at:
- **CR&R Facility**: 599 E. Main Street, El Centro, CA
